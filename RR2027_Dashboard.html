Methodology overview

From pre-delivery match state to player-level pressure performance.

01 · Define pressure

Pre-delivery context

Pressure is operationalised from match state before each delivery: innings context, phase, wickets/resources, target/par position and chase situation. No post-delivery information is used to define the state.

02 · Estimate impact

Contextual counterfactuals

Observed outcomes are compared with out-of-fold expected outcomes conditioned on delivery and match context. Wicket value is state-dependent; player residuals are shrunk to reduce small-sample noise.

03 · Rank cautiously

Composite performance

The report combines pressure impact, dot-ball contribution and match-to-match consistency, then uses match-cluster bootstrap intervals and Top 10 inclusion probability to show uncertainty.

Reliability, limitations & reproducibility

Important caveats for decision-makers.

What the analysis supports

Historical pressure-performance comparisons within the supplied 2022–2026 dataset; point ratings, bootstrap intervals, exposure counts, inferred roles and full-pool candidate screening.

What it does not support

Current player availability, contractual status, future performance, auction prices, official role designation, or a definitive ranking of closely clustered players.

Key limitations

  • Modest HP sample sizes and overlapping uncertainty intervals.
  • Extras schema cannot directly reconstruct multi-run overthrows on wides/byes; prior audit bounded economy-rate distortion at up to 0.12 RPO.
  • IPL/T20I format translation may be imperfect.
  • Pressure is a pre-delivery operational proxy, not a psychological measure.
  • Roles are inferred from observed usage, not official designations.
Reproducibility disclosure: The companion analysis.py uses seed 20261113, but its current output does not exactly reproduce the leaderboard printed in the report. The script and source CSVs are supplied for audit/rerunning; exact end-to-end reproduction is not claimed without the matching frozen code/configuration snapshot.
